Spacer Support

Help with the game, and how to reach me

Something wrong, or a question not answered here? Write to mark.tsiareshka@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps.

My steps aren't counting

Spacer reads steps from Apple Health, so first check that the Health app shows them. Then check that Spacer may read them: in the Health app, open your profile › Apps › Spacer and turn on Steps. Steps from an Apple Watch or another app count once they reach Health.

When is a day decided?

At midnight. A day short of its goal waits until 03:00 for steps still syncing from a watch. If it is still short, the reserve tank covers what it can, then a shield saves the ship. Without either, the ship and its captain are lost.

How do I change my goal?

At the end of each week your captain looks back with you. You can keep the goal or ease it, and raise it after a week with every goal met. The new goal starts the next day.

How do I stop the reminders?

Spacer warns you in the evening when today's goal is still out of reach, and tells you in the morning where the ship has arrived. Turn them off in Settings › Notifications › Spacer.

Why can't I talk to my captain?

Calls need Apple Intelligence: an iPhone 15 Pro or newer on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Right after it is turned on, it may need a while to get ready. The captain's answers are written by an AI model on your iPhone, so they can be wrong. The game's own screens are always right.

Where is my journey saved?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account, so a journey can't be moved to another phone except through your iPhone's own backup. Deleting Spacer deletes the journey.

Privacy

Spacer collects nothing and never connects to the internet. Read the privacy policy.
