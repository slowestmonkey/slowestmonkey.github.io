Spacer Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Spacer collects no data. Your steps and your journey stay on your iPhone, and the app never connects to the internet.

Your steps

With your permission, Spacer reads your daily step count from Apple Health to fuel your ship. It only reads steps, never writes to Health, and reads nothing else. The count is used on your iPhone to play the game and is never sent anywhere, shared, sold or used for advertising.

You can turn off access at any time in the Health app, under your profile › Apps › Spacer.

Your journey

Your ship, daily goal and log are saved on your iPhone. Like other app data, they are included in your device backups to iCloud or your computer if you have those turned on, which Apple handles under your own settings. Deleting Spacer deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. No server sends them and nothing about you is sent to one.

The postcard

When you reach the Core, you can share or save a postcard of your journey. It only leaves the app when you choose to share it, and Spacer adds it to Photos only when you ask. Spacer cannot see your photo library.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Spacer has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising code, and does not track you across apps or websites. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on request, and this applies equally to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
